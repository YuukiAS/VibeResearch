## Purpose

Add a benchmark that proves the framework rejects low-value progress theater:
generic method search, clone-only work, smoke/metadata-only artifacts, renamed
old experiments, uncleared WATCH debt, orphan knowledge, role-boundary breaks,
and low-quota continuation.

- Define benchmark traps for low-value plans, negative-memory repeats,
  clone/install-only repo work, evidence promotion, WATCH debt, role boundaries,
  budget runtime, orphan knowledge, and registry duplicate blocking.

  registry duplicate blocking, decision debt clearing, evidence promotion,
  role-boundary compliance, and budget checkpoint/resume.
- Expose the benchmark through CLI without running CARE rounds or external
  network work.
- Keep test fixtures toy and deterministic.
