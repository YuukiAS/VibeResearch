# VibeResearch 0.19.1 Plan: No Orphan Knowledge

Source TODO: `docs/todos/TODO-v30.md`

## Purpose

Prevent repositories, papers, deep research notes, mechanism cards, and user
ideas from staying active as vague future potential. Knowledge must become an
active mechanism, negative evidence, archived reference, or expired orphan
within two cycles.

## Scope

- Define lifecycle statuses: `INGESTED`, `MECHANISM_CARD`, `PLAN_CANDIDATE`,
  `ACTIVE_MECHANISM`, `NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE`, `ARCHIVED_REFERENCE`, and
  `EXPIRED_ORPHAN`.
- Track knowledge items in an append-only lifecycle log with two-cycle TTL.
- Connect mechanism-card creation, Planner draft-from-card, and Compiler MVE
  compile to lifecycle updates.
- Clear unresolved active knowledge into `EXPIRED_ORPHAN` after two cycles and
  write the expiration to the Research Registry.
- Generate orphan audits with counts for new knowledge, mechanism-card
  conversion, archived references, negative evidence, and expired orphans.
- Keep this as framework/integration logic only.

## Files

- `vibe_research/knowledge_lifecycle.py`: lifecycle schema, event log, TTL
  clearing, active-mechanism updates, and orphan audit.
- `vibe_research/scout.py`: record lifecycle events when mechanism cards are
  created or archived.
- `vibe_research/planner.py`: mark card-backed drafts as plan candidates.
- `vibe_research/compiler.py`: mark card-backed compiled MVEs as active
  mechanisms.
- `vibe_research/cli.py`: knowledge lifecycle and orphan-audit commands.
- `tests/test_knowledge_lifecycle.py`: TODO-v30 coverage.
- `CHANGELOG.md`, `README.md`, `docs/README_CN.md`, and version metadata.
